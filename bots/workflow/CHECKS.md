# Recorded routing checks

Six hypothetical cases were exercised in the existing Firstmate conversation during setup on September 5, 2026. Each observed answer met the expected routing behavior:

- Simple rewrite: handle it directly and stop after delivering the draft.
- Five shop-owner interview notes and two weekends: choose Product Ideation for a problem and cheap test; other specialists are conditional.
- Code complexity review: choose Garbage Collector for review only, without edits.
- Disagreement about launch evidence: identify the disputed assumption and smallest check, without a debate loop.
- Social post draft: deliver the draft without publishing.
- Duplicate Bot names without verified identity: resolve identity before messaging, or prepare a handoff.

The saved workflow was inspected against its source after whitespace normalization. These were hypothetical routing responses, not completed specialist handoffs or a fresh-session test. Machine-specific Bot identities and setup transcripts are not included. No routing checks were rerun during the source migration.
